# FinGuard AI color palette

FinGuard AI uses its own midnight navy, cobalt, and coral identity. The open layout takes inspiration from [Feedzai's AI page](https://www.feedzai.com/solutions/ai/), while the colors, shield mark, and product content are FinGuard AI's.

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight navy | `#17203A` | Headings, primary buttons, announcement banner, decision panel |
| Cobalt | `#3F5FE0` | Interactive accents and selected states |
| Coral | `#FF9B83` | Brand highlight inside the dark decision panel and shield mark |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Main canvas and cards |
| Ice | `#F5F7FC` | Secondary surfaces and navigation |
| Pale blue | `#E8EEFF` | Feature cards and soft accent areas |
| Pale peach | `#FFF0EA` | Third feature card |
| Slate | `#59647A` | Supporting text |
| Border | `#DDE3F0` | Dividers and controls |

Risk states use separate semantic colors: **allow** `#315D24`, **warn** `#85510A`, **hold** `#A02E30`, and **ask** `#3B5A74`. This keeps the coral brand accent distinct from a hold decision. The dark theme is defined in `web/src/styles.css`.
